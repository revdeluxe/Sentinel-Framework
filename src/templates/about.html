Sentinel is a biometric identity and attendance platform designed for controlled access, user self-service, and workforce timekeeping. The current deployment mode is {{ mode|title }}.

Privacy and data handling
  • Face embeddings are stored as biometric templates for matching and enrollment.
  • User settings are scoped to the signed-in account, and admin tools are restricted by role.
  • Attendance and biometric events are recorded as audit logs for traceability.
  • Use HTTPS, role-based access, retention limits, and consent flows in production deployments.
ISO-aligned controls
  • Access control and least privilege for administrative actions.
  • Auditability for identity, enrollment, and attendance events.
  • Configuration separation between deployment modes and user-facing features.
  • Operational review for privacy, retention, and incident response before production use.
